Important cross-section interpretations

S1 + S8 — Approval is not opening authorization. Committee approval in principle does not satisfy either operational prerequisite. Both storage confirmation and checklist approval must be completed before the opening announcement.

S3 + S9 — Emergency status is not a booking. The exception changes only the minimum request lead time. It does not guarantee same-day service, waive other requirements, or establish available inventory.

S4 + S7 — Physical handover is not completed return. A kit delivered to an unrelated volunteer outside desk hours remains outside the completed return process. Inspection and discrepancy resolution are distinct stages.

S5 + S7 — Deposit review is not an established penalty. Missing items, damage, or disagreement trigger inspection or review procedures, but the handbook establishes neither automatic deductions nor specific charges.

S2 + S8 — Eligibility and capacity are separate. An accepted registration and two planned kits do not establish availability for a particular event.

S6 + S3 — Compatibility should be addressed before confirmation. Even successful collection checks do not guarantee compatibility with every device or presentation.

5. Overall Decision Assessment

Borrowing next week cannot be confirmed from this handbook.

The decisive blocker is not simply whether the group qualifies or whether one of the two planned kits is free. The pilot's opening prerequisites remain unfulfilled in the supplied version, with no confirmed opening date.

A legitimate booking would require opening authorization, accepted group registration, compliance with the ordinary notice period or an approved emergency exception, confirmed stock, agreed collection and return appointments, written confirmation, and disclosure of applicable charges before acceptance.
