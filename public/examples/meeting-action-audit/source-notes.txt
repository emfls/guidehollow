AUTHOR'S REVIEW RULES
- Extract only what the approved minutes authorize or explicitly state.
- An offer, suggestion, cost estimate, or draft agenda item is not an approved action.
- An action can be approved while its owner or date remains unspecified.
- Keep the storage condition attached to Lena's scheduling action.
- A meeting date is not automatically a deadline for every task.
- Do not convert a relative deadline into a different calendar date.
- Use the separate answer key only after completing the blind worksheet.
